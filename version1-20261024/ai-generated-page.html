01 / INDEPENDENT PROJECT · 2024–2025

A small car.
A complete system.

From circuit design and assembly to programming and full-vehicle testing: an intelligent car built to explore sensing, feedback and motion.

  • C++
  • Arduino
  • PID control
  • Computer vision
Inside the project +

Hardware to motion

Built and tested an H-bridge motor driver. Used wheel-encoder feedback for fixed-distance movement, square-path driving and in-place rotation, with I²C and UART connecting peripheral modules.

Sense, test, tune

Implemented two-sensor and eight-channel line tracking, then tuned PID parameters to reduce oscillation, cornering overshoot and track deviation. Extended the platform with camera-based visual processing and tested the integrated system.

Additional modules: battery monitoring, rotary-encoder input, timer-generated signals and MPU6050 motion sensing.
